# Technology

The history of technology studies techniques as human knowledge in practice: tools, crafts, machines, infrastructures, and the societies that make and use them. It asks how techniques are born, how they spread, and how they reshape work, war, and daily life.

The [Technology Atlas](atlas.html) maps this history on an old world map: places of origin, a timeline from stone tools to large language models, and the cumulative stock of techniques known to humankind.
