The birth of technology

Technology begins before history: shaped stone, tamed fire and planted seed — tools, energy and food.

c. 3,300,000 BCE · East Africa

Shaped stone

Flaked edges cut meat and wood. The oldest known tools predate our own genus; technique comes before humanity as we know it.

c. 1,000,000 BCE · hearths worldwide

Tamed fire

Cooking, warmth, light and protection. Fire extends the day, unlocks calories and gathers people around the hearth.

c. 10,000 BCE · Fertile Crescent

Planted seed

Domesticated plants and animals feed villages, then cities. Farming creates surplus — and with it, priests, scribes and kings.
