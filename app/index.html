Laboratorium Maius Studiorum Historiologiae

Hispanic history, culture & letters, in notes.

Historiologia is a collaborative research hub for the advanced study, preservation and dissemination of Hispanic cultural, historical and literary heritage, with comparative notes on the wider world. It is a working corpus: notes are written, cited and revised in the open.

— notes — words

Method

Each topic is a note — a markdown file rendered on the fly, titled by its own first heading, linked to primary sources and other notes. Interactive pages are live notes (self-contained HTML) such as the atlas, which shows the corpus by volume. Every note shares the same navigation, typography and theme; the catalog is the navigator, with full-text search, section and tag facets, and a filter for notes still in progress. Read more →
